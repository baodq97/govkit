ERP sync — operational notes
============================

The ERP is the box in the corner that finance still runs on. We only ever READ
from it, once a night, over its SOAP interface.

Hard truths about it:
  - We do not own it and cannot get changes made to it. The team that built the
    integration is long gone; the vendor quotes six figures to touch anything.
  - Its export format is inconsistent. Field names have changed between releases
    (ASSET_NO vs assetNo), nulls show up where they shouldn't, and occasionally a
    batch arrives with columns in a different order.
  - When it misbehaves, it must not be allowed to corrupt our own asset records.

Because of all that, the sync job keeps the ERP's mess quarantined: it reads the
raw dump, translates it into our own clean shapes, and only then hands it on.
Nothing past the sync job ever sees a raw ERP field. If the ERP changes again,
only the sync job should need touching.
