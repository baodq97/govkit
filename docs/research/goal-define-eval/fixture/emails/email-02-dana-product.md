Subject: my take on the feedback build — please read before you start

I know Marcus is going to push hard for the text-message version. I want to put
the counter-view on record before anyone writes code, because I think the short
SMS blast is exactly how we get ourselves in trouble and burn the merchant's
relationship with their own customer.

Here's where I land:

- It should go out over **email**, not text. Email is where we can be tasteful,
  branded, and give the recipient context for why they're hearing from us.
- One number on a 1–5 scale tells us almost nothing. I want a **few questions** —
  the rating, a reason, and an open comment box — so the merchant gets something
  they can actually act on. A lonely "3" is useless.
- And critically, people should have **clearly agreed to hear from us** before we
  send anything. A real opt-in, confirmed, on the record. I don't want us blasting
  people who never said yes. That's how you become the annoying brand.

I get that this is slower and the response numbers will look worse than Marcus's
one-tap text. I think that's the right trade. We are building trust infrastructure;
if the first thing we do is spam people, the whole "close the loop" story dies.

Marcus and I have gone back and forth on this and honestly we're not converging.
Somebody who isn't one of us needs to make the call on which way v1 goes, because
we can't build both and hit the timeline.

p.s. saw Marcus's note about SendGrid going away end of August — yes, that's
real, the SES path is the only one that survives the cutover, please design for
that from the start.
