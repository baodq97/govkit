## Problem

Customers cannot see their metered usage until the monthly invoice arrives, which
drives bill-shock support tickets and erodes trust in the metering itself.

## Persona

A finance owner at a mid-market SaaS buyer who reconciles spend weekly and needs to
attribute cost to teams before the invoice lands.

## Success metrics

Reduce bill-shock support tickets by 40% within one quarter and lift weekly active
dashboard usage to 60% of paying accounts.

## Scope

In scope: usage charts, per-meter breakdown, CSV export. Non-goals: forecasting and
real-time alerting (tracked separately).
