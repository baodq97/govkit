## Summary

We will improve the webhook dispatch system using modern best practices. The new design is
scalable, robust, and maintainable, and will significantly improve reliability and
performance across the board for all of our users and integrations.

## Alternatives and trade-offs

Several alternatives were considered and rejected because they were less optimal. The chosen
approach is preferred rather than the others because it aligns better with our goals,
follows industry standards, and provides a more solid foundation for future growth.

## Impact and rollout

The rollout will be smooth and incremental. Impact on existing users is expected to be
minimal, and the migration will follow established best practices to ensure a seamless
transition with high quality throughout.

## Open questions

There may be some edge cases to consider as the work proceeds, and the team will address
them as they come up.

## Recommendation

Adopt the improved design, which delivers scalability, reliability, robustness, and
maintainability, and positions the system for long-term success.
